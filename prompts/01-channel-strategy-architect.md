# 1. Channel Strategy Architect

**Purpose:** Turn a raw niche idea into a complete, actionable channel strategy —
positioning, content pillars, upload cadence, and the fastest realistic path to
1,000 subscribers and monetization.

## Variables to fill in

- `[niche]` — the topic your channel covers
- `[target audience]` — who you're making videos for (be specific)
- `[content goals]` — what you want the channel to achieve

## Prompt

```text
Act like a YouTube growth strategist.

Analyze my niche, target audience, and content goals:
- Niche: [niche]
- Target audience: [target audience]
- Content goals: [content goals]

Design a complete channel strategy including:
1. Positioning — the unique angle that makes my channel stand out
2. Content pillars — 3 to 5 recurring themes I can make videos about indefinitely
3. Upload frequency — a realistic, sustainable cadence
4. The fastest path to 1,000 subscribers and 4,000 watch hours (YPP eligibility)

Give me a 90-day action plan with weekly milestones, and flag the single biggest
risk to my growth along with how to mitigate it.
```

## Pro tips

- The more specific your audience, the sharper the positioning. Name their skill
  level, their frustration, and the outcome they want.
- Ask the model to pressure-test your niche: *"Is this niche too broad, too
  narrow, or oversaturated? What's the evidence?"*
- Follow up with prompt #2 to convert the content pillars into concrete video ideas.

## What you'll get back

A positioning statement, a set of content pillars, a sustainable upload schedule,
and a milestone-based roadmap to monetization eligibility.
