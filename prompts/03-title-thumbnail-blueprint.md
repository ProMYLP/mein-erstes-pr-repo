# 3. Title & Thumbnail Blueprint

**Purpose:** Package a single video idea for maximum click-through rate — 10
irresistible titles, each paired with a thumbnail concept engineered to make
scrolling past feel impossible.

## Variables to fill in

- `[topic]` — the subject of the specific video

## Prompt

```text
Act like a YouTube click-through rate (CTR) expert.

Write 10 irresistible video titles for a video about [topic], and describe the
ideal thumbnail concept for each.

Every title must:
- Trigger curiosity (open a loop the viewer needs to close)
- Promise a clear, specific benefit
- Make scrolling past feel impossible

For each thumbnail, describe:
- The main visual / focal point
- The facial expression or emotion (if a person is shown)
- The 2 to 4 words of on-thumbnail text
- The dominant color and contrast choice

Then pick the single strongest title + thumbnail pairing and explain why it will
out-click the rest.
```

## Pro tips

- Titles and thumbnails work as a *pair* — never optimize one without the other.
- Ask for variations at different "curiosity levels" so you can A/B test a bold
  hook against a straightforward one.
- Keep on-thumbnail text to 4 words or fewer; it has to be readable at phone size.

## What you'll get back

10 title + thumbnail pairings with concrete visual direction, plus a
recommendation on the highest-CTR combination.
