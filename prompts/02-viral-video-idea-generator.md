# 2. Viral Video Idea Generator

**Purpose:** Generate a batch of 20 high-potential video ideas grounded in search
demand, trending topics, and real audience pain points — each with a title, hook
angle, and a reason it can spread.

## Variables to fill in

- `[niche]` — the topic area you want ideas for

## Prompt

```text
Act like a YouTube content researcher.

Generate 20 high-potential video ideas for [niche], based on search demand,
trending topics, and audience pain points.

For each idea, include:
- Working title
- Hook angle (the first-line promise that stops the scroll)
- Why it has viral potential (search demand, emotion, timeliness, or shareability)

Group the ideas into 3 buckets:
1. Search-driven (evergreen, steady traffic)
2. Trend-driven (timely, spikes fast)
3. Audience-driven (solves a specific pain point)

Rank all 20 by potential and tell me which 3 I should make first, and why.
```

## Pro tips

- Feed the content pillars from prompt #1 straight into this one for ideas that
  reinforce your positioning.
- Ask for the *search intent* behind each idea so you know what the viewer
  actually wants before you script it.
- Re-run monthly to keep a running backlog — never start a video from a blank page.

## What you'll get back

20 ideas sorted into evergreen, trending, and pain-point buckets, ranked by
potential, with a clear recommendation on what to film first.
