# 6. Community Growth System

**Purpose:** Build a repeatable daily 15-minute routine for engaging with comments,
collaborating with fellow creators, and turning casual viewers into a loyal audience
that watches — and shares — every video.

## Variables to fill in

- `[niche]` — optional; add it to make collaboration and engagement tactics specific

## Prompt

```text
Act like a YouTube community strategist.

Design a daily 15-minute routine for building a loyal, engaged audience[ in the
[niche] niche]. Break the 15 minutes into a concrete, timed checklist covering:

1. Comment engagement — how to reply in a way that boosts the comment section and
   the algorithm, and which comments to prioritize
2. Creator collaboration — how to find and build relationships with similar-sized
   creators for shoutouts, collabs, and cross-promotion
3. Loyalty building — tactics that turn one-time viewers into subscribers who watch
   every upload and share content organically

Also give me:
- A weekly version of the routine for higher-leverage actions (community posts,
  polls, collab outreach)
- 3 metrics to track so I know the system is working
```

## Pro tips

- Reply to comments hardest in the first 60 minutes after publishing — early
  engagement signals help the algorithm push the video.
- Collaboration compounds: one shoutout from a creator your size can outperform
  weeks of solo posting.
- Pin a comment that asks a question — it seeds the conversation and lifts your
  engagement rate.

## What you'll get back

A timed 15-minute daily checklist, a weekly high-leverage routine, and the metrics
to confirm your community is actually growing.
