# 5. YouTube SEO Optimizer

**Purpose:** Maximize a video's discoverability on YouTube search, suggested feed,
and Google — by optimizing the title, description, tags, and chapter markers around
the right keywords.

## Variables to fill in

- `[topic]` — the subject of the video you're publishing

## Prompt

```text
Act like a YouTube SEO specialist.

Optimize the metadata for a video about [topic]. Deliver:

1. Title — SEO-strong but still click-worthy (front-load the primary keyword)
2. Description — a compelling first 2 lines, a keyword-rich full description,
   and placeholders for links/timestamps
3. Tags — a prioritized list from broad to specific
4. Chapter markers — timestamped chapters using searchable phrasing

Also identify:
- The highest-ranking primary keyword and 3 secondary keywords
- Semantic / related phrases YouTube associates with this topic
- The search patterns and intent behind those keywords

Explain your keyword choices briefly so I understand the reasoning.
```

## Pro tips

- The first 1–2 lines of the description are what shows in search — treat them like
  ad copy, not filler.
- Chapters double as keyword real estate *and* improve retention; phrase them the
  way people search.
- Balance is everything: a keyword-stuffed title that no one clicks will lose to a
  human one. Optimize for CTR first, SEO second.

## What you'll get back

A copy-paste-ready title, description, tag list, and chapter set — plus the keyword
research and reasoning behind each choice.
