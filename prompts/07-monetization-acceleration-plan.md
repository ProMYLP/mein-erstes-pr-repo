# 7. Monetization Acceleration Plan

**Purpose:** Design a complete revenue strategy for your channel — one that starts
generating income *before* you hit 10,000 subscribers, across AdSense, sponsorships,
digital products, and affiliates.

## Variables to fill in

- `[niche]` — your channel's topic
- `[subscriber count]` — where you are today

## Prompt

```text
Act like a YouTube monetization expert.

Design a complete monetization strategy for a channel in the [niche] niche with
[subscriber count] subscribers.

Cover four revenue streams:
1. AdSense optimization — content length, ad placement, and format choices that
   raise RPM
2. Sponsorships — how to find, pitch, and price brand deals at my current size,
   including a short outreach template
3. Digital products — what I could realistically create and sell to this audience
4. Affiliate opportunities — relevant programs and how to integrate them naturally

Prioritize the streams by how fast they can generate revenue at my subscriber
count, and give me a 30-day plan to launch the first one. Flag which streams to
ignore until I'm larger, and why.
```

## Pro tips

- Sponsorships and digital products usually pay off long before AdSense does — don't
  wait for the Partner Program to start earning.
- Recommend affiliate products you'd genuinely use; audience trust is the asset that
  makes every other stream work.
- Ask for realistic revenue ranges at your subscriber count so your expectations
  stay grounded.

## What you'll get back

A prioritized, four-stream monetization plan with a pricing and outreach template
for sponsors, product ideas, affiliate matches, and a 30-day launch plan for your
first revenue stream.
