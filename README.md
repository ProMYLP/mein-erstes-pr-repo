# 🎬 YouTube Strategy Toolkit

A collection of 7 battle-tested prompt templates that turn Claude (or any capable
LLM) into a full YouTube growth team — strategist, researcher, copywriter,
scriptwriter, SEO specialist, community manager, and monetization advisor.

Copy a prompt, fill in the `[bracketed]` variables, and paste it into your AI
assistant. Each prompt is self-contained, but they're designed to chain together
into a complete workflow (see [Workflow](#-the-workflow)).

## 🔄 The Workflow

The prompts follow the natural lifecycle of a growing channel. Run them in order,
feeding the output of each into the next:

```
1. Channel Strategy Architect      →  Decide your niche, pillars, and cadence
        ↓
2. Viral Video Idea Generator      →  Turn your pillars into 20 concrete ideas
        ↓
3. Title & Thumbnail Blueprint     →  Package the best ideas for maximum CTR
        ↓
4. Video Script Builder            →  Write scripts that keep viewers watching
        ↓
5. YouTube SEO Optimizer           →  Make each video discoverable
        ↓
6. Community Growth System         →  Convert viewers into loyal subscribers
        ↓
7. Monetization Acceleration Plan  →  Turn the audience into revenue
```

---

## 🛠️ How to Use

1. Open the prompt file you need.
2. Replace every `[bracketed placeholder]` with your own details
   (niche, audience, topic, subscriber count, etc.).
3. Paste the finished prompt into Claude, ChatGPT, or your LLM of choice.
4. Iterate — ask follow-up questions, request variations, or push back on
   anything that doesn't fit your channel.

> **Tip:** The more specific your inputs, the sharper the output. "Beginner home
> cooks who feel intimidated by knife skills" beats "cooking audience" every time.
